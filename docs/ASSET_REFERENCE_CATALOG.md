# LabSpace authored-asset reference catalog

## How the references are used

The user-supplied Kyushu University Room 809 photographs and the later product-style bench,
wash-basin, island, storage, drying-rack, freezer, and rotary references are the primary visual sources for proportions,
finishes, wear, cabling, storage patterns, and dense laboratory context. The official sources below supplement those references
only where product anatomy or a hidden side must be inferred: controls, vents,
door construction, access panels, clearances, and service connections.

The July 18 Lab Modular Asset Pack sheets are composition and family references,
not dimension authorities. `Lab Bench Shimadzu Ref2.png` is the primary supplied
composition reference for the double-sided island: light two-sided casework,
dark phenolic working surfaces, end drawer banks, central cabinets, service
spine, and raised three-bay glazed sliding-door hutch. Official sources remain
authoritative for equipment envelopes and functional anatomy.

No manufacturer mesh, CAD drawing, logo, label, catalog photograph, or catalog
texture is redistributed. The resulting models are original, editable planning
representations and are not manufacturer-certified digital twins.

## Room 809 demonstration layout reference

The five supplied Floorplanner captures dated 2026-07-15 are the required spatial reference for the optional Room 809 demo:

- `Screenshot 2026-07-15 152427.png` is the canonical top-plan reference for the measured footprint, entrance recess, wall openings, circulation, two island benches, perimeter benching, sinks, cold storage, racks, and the rotary-evaporator row.
- `Screenshot 2026-07-15 152631.png`, `152658.png`, `152715.png`, and `152729.png` are the four complementary orbit references for orientation, relative placement, and clearance checking.
- Their simple Floorplanner furniture and equipment are geometry placeholders, not the visual-quality target. LabSpace should preserve their placement intent while substituting the detailed, material-aware authored assets documented below.
- These captures govern the Room 809 demonstration only. New rooms remain blank and the professional editor remains reusable across unrelated laboratories.

Implementation status (2026-07-18): the demonstration now uses an eight-segment concave shell with an 8.71 × 8.69 m outer envelope, 68.611 m² floor, 34.8 m perimeter, projected main entrance, hosted 2.20 m double door, hosted 0.90 m service door, and five hosted 0.95 m north windows. Its 63 scene objects include two long authored service-bridge islands, six authored rotary evaporators on the north run, and the referenced perimeter casework, sinks, cold storage, racks, equipment, seating, and circulation. These dimensions are carefully inferred from the screenshots and remain planning values until checked against a measured survey.

## Procedural families awaiting authored migration

The remaining 51 procedural entries include utilities, safety equipment,
additional cabinetry, carts, containers, and other laboratory objects. Eleven
professional doors/windows intentionally remain dimension-driven parametric
assemblies so their frame, leaf, pane, track, handing, sill and wall-thickness
geometry can follow the hosted opening. Other category-specific fallbacks remain
suitable for planning, but high-use laboratory equipment still requires a
manufacturer-informed, all-sided authored GLB before it can be treated as part
of the photoreal hero library.

All authored dimensions remain editable planning defaults and must not be used
as certified installation drawings, clearance requirements, or procurement data.
