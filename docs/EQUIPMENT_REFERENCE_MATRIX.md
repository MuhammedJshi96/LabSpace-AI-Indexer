# Room 809 equipment reference matrix

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This matrix is a living modeling brief for authored GLB passes. It does **not**
identify the photographed Room 809 units with certainty, and it does not claim
that the current LabSpace assets are manufacturer-exact digital twins. The Room
809 photographs remain the authority for visible colour, wear, accessories,
cable and hose clutter, and installed context; `references/ref1.png` and
`references/ref2.png` remain the target for whole-room density and finish. The
official sources below are representative anatomy and dimension references for
parts that are obscured in the photographs.

Dimensions are manufacturer envelope dimensions in **W × D × H** order unless
noted. Protruding handles, ducts, plugs, hoses and open doors/lids must be stored
as a separate clearance envelope when the source excludes them. Every asset
should carry `referenceStatus: representative` until a Room 809 nameplate and
field measurements confirm the exact model.

## Authoring and review rules

1. Build every item as closed, orbitable geometry with credible front, rear,
   sides, top and underside. Flat facade cards and billboard backs fail review.
2. Store a nominal equipment envelope, an operational envelope (open lid/sash/
   door), and any installation clearance separately. Do not bake a clearance
   box into visible geometry.
3. Use shared PBR material families: powder-coated steel, brushed SUS304,
   moulded polymer, optical/tempered glass, rubber gasket/hose, painted cast
   metal, resin worktop and laminate. Decals should be original generic labels,
   not copied manufacturer artwork.
4. Unknown hidden faces stay restrained: service-panel seams, ventilation and
   cable exits may be inferred only where the official anatomy makes them
   necessary. Exact fastener patterns, certification stickers and connector
   layouts wait for the matching manual or Room 809 field evidence.
5. Generate the Asset Library isometric thumbnail and 2D material-aware top
   view from the same GLB and material set used in 3D. A separate geometric icon
   is fallback-only and is not acceptable for these priority assets.
6. Before an asset is called “Room 809 exact,” capture its nameplate/model,
   field W/D/H, door or lid swing, service-side photos and at least one rear/top
   view; then replace the `representative` metadata and record the evidence.
