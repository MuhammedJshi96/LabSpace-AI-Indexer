# Targeted furniture refinement — 2026-08-30

Scope: mobile bench, office desk, standard lab bench, open stainless wash basin,
computer workstation, and corner laboratory bench. Unrelated catalog materials
and the shared runtime material treatment remain unchanged.

## Reference packet

- User's annotated Asset Studio screenshots identify disconnected support frames,
  floating worktops, poorly resolved corner casework, and an overly dark wash basin.
- [Herman Miller Everywhere specifications](https://www.hermanmiller.com/products/workspaces/collaborative-furniture/everywhere-tables/specs/)
  inform the workstation's laminate worktop, connected T-leg structure, levelling
  glides, and below-surface cable management. Its existing 1400 × 700 mm footprint
  is retained; this is not a replica or a manufacturer-certified product.
- [Kewaunee steel casework](https://kewaunee.com/product/steel-casework/) and
  [construction specification, pp. 7–9](https://kewaunee.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/steel-spec-2016-03.pdf)
  inform folded gables, flush faces, rear construction, and recessed toe space.
  The corner model retains its 1500 × 1500 × 900 mm envelope and uses a single
  continuous L-shaped worktop with joined perpendicular cabinet runs.
- Existing Room 809 references continue to govern the light-neutral casework,
  restrained satin hardware, and black phenolic worktop palette.

All geometry is authored locally from original code, including conservative rear
and underside construction. No third-party meshes, logos, or product textures
were imported. Models and both catalog/plan renders are regenerated together.
The wash basin's revised finish is authored only in its own GLB; no global
lighting or material override is used to correct that asset.
